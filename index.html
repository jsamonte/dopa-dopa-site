Dopa-Dopa

Hovercraft racing for Meta Quest, with your bare hands or Touch controllers: 16 wild tracks, power-ups, and online races for up to 8 players.

Support

Something not working, or a question about the game? Email jared.v.samonte@gmail.com and say which headset you play on and what happened.

  • Hold the Meta button to put the cockpit back in front of you.
  • Feeling dizzy? Turn the COMFORT setting up in the track select.
  • Online races need an internet connection; friends who press PLAY ONLINE at about the same time race together.
