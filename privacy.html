Dopa-Dopa

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Dopa-Dopa is a hovercraft racing game for Meta Quest made by the Dopa-Dopa team ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, how it is used, and how to ask us to delete it. We do not sell personal information, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or ask you to make an account of our own: the game uses the Meta account you are already signed in to on your headset.

What stays on your headset

The game saves your settings (comfort, colour, minimap), the prizes you have unlocked, and a small performance log, in the game's own storage on your headset. They never leave the headset. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What the game sends during online races

Only when you choose PLAY ONLINE, INVITE FRIENDS or join a friend (or join a race server on your own network), the game sends the race server:

  • your headset's IP address, which any internet connection needs, also used to pick a server near you;
  • your racer name, shown to the other racers: your Meta display name, or where the game cannot get it, a name made from the last four characters of an ID that Android gives the game on your headset (for example "Quest 1A2B");
  • your hovercraft's position and movement, its colour and theme, your track votes, and the power-ups you use.

This is used only to run the race you are in and show it to the other players in it. Online race servers are hosted for us by Edgegap (edgegap.com), which processes your IP address to connect you and choose a nearby server. We keep none of it: a race server shuts down soon after its race is over. Edgegap may keep the server's technical logs, which can include racer names and IP addresses, for a short time under Edgegap's privacy policy.

Meta

When you buy and play Dopa-Dopa through the Meta Horizon Store, the game asks Meta's platform to confirm that your account owns it. Meta's own handling of your data is described in Meta's privacy policy.

The game also uses these Meta platform features, all on your headset:

  • your Meta user ID and display name, to use the display name as your racer name;
  • group presence: while you are in an online race lobby, Meta shows your friends that you are in Dopa-Dopa's online lobby and lets them join you. The lobby is identified by the address of the race server you are in. Leaving the lobby clears it;
  • invites: INVITE FRIENDS opens Meta's own invite panel, which lists your Meta friends. The game does not read, keep or send your friends list;
  • joining: when you accept a friend's invite or choose Join on their status, Meta tells the game which race server your friend is in, and the game joins it.

None of this is stored by the game or by us.

Children

The game does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what an online race needs to run, described above, and keeps none of it.

Deleting your data, and questions

Everything the game keeps is on your headset: uninstall the game to delete it. We hold nothing else about you, but if you have a question or a request about your data, email jared.v.samonte@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
